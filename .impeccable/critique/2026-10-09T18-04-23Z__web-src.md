## Overall Impression

A big step up from 26/40. Kid mode and the Tomorrow board are now the product at its best, and the shouting from the first critique (banners, kid mode, admin prose) is gone. The biggest remaining opportunity is **Today itself**. It still leads with alerts rather than the day, so the one urgent thing has to compete with four others.

## What's Working

1. **Kid mode is the best-designed screen.** Clock faces stand in for times, "Dad takes you" has a face, moons sit in fives, and the tiles are big and stamp on. It meets "Kids are real users" in full.
2. **The Tomorrow board's "Needs sorting".** Each gap becomes a question you answer in place. At night it follows the Bedside Rule all the way.
3. **Color discipline.** Person color carries identity, shared events stay neutral, and categories are emoji, so clashes show at a glance in Week and Month.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Hub Today: alerts outrank the day**
- **Why it matters:**
  - Up to six heads-up cards, five with solid navy pills, sit above the timeline.
  - On a phone, "Next 6 hours" starts about two screens down.
  - A live leave-by shows as a card, a timeline pill and a banner at once.
  - It breaks "Glance, don't study", and the one urgent item loses its edge.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep only urgent (bad) alerts as cards. Fold the rest into one row of compact chips (🚦 3 rides · ✅ 3 tasks · 💵 Hydro · 🎒 Pack), each opening its list.
  - Hide the leave-by card while its nudge banner is showing.
  - Make non-urgent actions quiet buttons.
  - On phones, put the timeline first.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill (then /impeccable layout)

**[P1] A kid tapping her face on the hub lands on a parent page**
- **Why it matters:**
  - Hub Today's faces link to /person/:id (Today.tsx:159), a text agenda.
  - The picture view sits behind a text-only "Open kid mode" button.
  - PRODUCT.md says kids tap their face to see their day, and Emma can't read the way in.
- **Fix:**
  - On a hub session, a kid's face opens /kid/:id directly.
  - Parents' faces keep their page.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt

**[P1] Quick add misreads input without saying so**
- **Why it matters:**
  - "Emma dentist Tuesday 3:30pm, Dad drives" previews as "Dentist , drives", with Dad added as a passenger and no driver.
  - "bring goggles" ends up in the title, not the bring note.
  - It's the fastest path and the one a distracted parent trusts.
- **Fix:**
  - Parse "<parent> drives/driving" into the driver and "bring X" into the packing note.
  - Strip stray punctuation from titles.
  - Add tests in shared/test/quickadd.test.ts.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] Plan sheet: always-open editors and a one-tap delete**
- **Why it matters:**
  - Every task always shows a raw date field, an assignee select and a bare ✕ (PlanSheet.tsx:48–71), about 330px per task on a phone.
  - "Due tomorrow" shows next to "2026-10-10".
  - The ✕ deletes with no confirm or undo and reads like "unassign".
- **Fix:**
  - Rows are check, emoji, title, due chip and face. Tapping a row opens its editors.
  - Remove goes through ConfirmButton or an undo toast.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

**[P2] Kid mode glows at night; locked Settings breaks One Lift**
- **Why it matters:**
  - Kid mode stays full white with person colors after night mode starts. That's the brightest screen in a dark kitchen, against the Bedside Rule.
  - Locked Settings (Settings.tsx:30–35) nests a shadowed panel inside a panel.
- **Fix:**
  - Apply the bedside token swap to kid mode at night too, or show a calm "Sleep time 🌙" screen.
  - Render locked Settings as a plain stack.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

## Minor Observations

- "Break into tasks" is the biggest tinted card in every event sheet, even for a dentist visit or School. A quiet link would do.
- The dark-mode berry heads-up card is heavy, and its white pills glow.
- On a phone, chore rows wrap 3+1. A 2×2 grid would read better.
- In kid mode on a phone, a 12-sleep moon row wraps mid-group.
- The hub's Week shows both "Drag to move" and "On a touch screen, tap an event…" (the hub is a touch screen).
- "Leave now" stays up 5 minutes after the leave-by time without saying it's late.
- `.welcome h1` and `.tb-head h1` use their own clamps instead of the Headline role.
- The 20px and 22px radii are off the named ladder.
- The kid "now" dot pulses forever. Check that it stops under reduced motion.
