## Priority Issues

**[P1] "Late" counts from a hidden 10-minute buffer** *(product rule from the start)*
- **What:** `leaveByMin` subtracts travel plus 10 minutes (shared/flags.ts). A ride starting in 6 minutes with a 15-minute drive reads "19 min late"; the real lateness is about 9 minutes.
- **Why it matters:** parents will check the arithmetic, find it wrong, and stop trusting the clock. It also turns red too early, which is nagging.
- **Fix:** either show the buffer ("15 min drive + 10 to get out the door"), or count "late" from start − travel and keep the buffer only for when the nudge fires.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P1] A done plan task says "3 days overdue"** *(pre-existing, carried through the plan-sheet change)*
- **What:** PlanSheet.tsx:51 and its aria-label use `dueLabel` whether or not the task is done.
- **Why it matters:** it's a scolding falsehood on a finished item.
- **Fix:** done tasks show "Done", or nothing.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P1] The phone roll-up leads with someone else's ride, and the screen-reader text is stale** *(from the banner work)*
- **What:**
  - Banners sort by leave time for everyone, so Mom's phone shows Dad's ride first and hides hers under "3 more".
  - The live region announces `nudges[0]`'s server title, not the first banner shown or its live wording.
- **Why it matters:** Mom sees the wrong person's emergency first, and a screen-reader user hears a different, frozen headline.
- **Fix:** on a parent's own device, sort their own nudges first, and announce the first shown banner's live text.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] One leave-by shows three times, all red**
- **What:** the banner, the timeline pill and the heads-up card or chip all repeat the same ride. On the hub, four banners stack over the middle of the timeline.
- **Fix:**
  - While a banner covers a ride, keep the pill quiet.
  - On the hub, cap the stack at two plus "+2 more", as phones already roll up.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable quieter

**[P2] Quick add and the plan sheet are overloaded and inconsistent**
- **What:**
  - Quick add shows about 30 choices under a correct parse.
  - The move of "Saturday" to Oct 17 has no "next Saturday" cue.
  - The plan sheet's two date pickers format days differently.
- **Fix:**
  - Fold Kind and Picture behind the parsed summary.
  - Add a "next Saturday" cue.
  - Use `dayWithDate` in both pickers.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill
