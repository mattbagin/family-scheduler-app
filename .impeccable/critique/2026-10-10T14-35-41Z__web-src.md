## Priority Issues

**[P0] Raw decimals in the late alert** (introduced in the last polish)
- **What:** Today.tsx:95 prints `-mins`. The current time includes seconds, so the alert reads "Leave now for Swim lesson: 1.1166666666666742 min late", and it wraps to three lines on a phone.
- **Why it matters:** it's in the reddest card, read in a rush, and it makes the whole screen look broken at the exact moment it matters.
- **Fix:** format with `fmtDur` (which rounds), and compare against whole minutes so "now" can actually occur.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P1] Urgency collapses once the leave-by nudge fires**
- **What:**
  - The urgent card is hidden while the banner shows (by design), but the banner looks just like the morning briefing banner (white, same size, same navy Got it).
  - The timeline's "Dad driving · leave 10:31" pill stays green after 10:31.
  - Kid mode still shows "Right now: Free time!" while Dad should be heading out.
- **Fix:**
  - Give an overdue or imminent leave-by banner the berry treatment ("Leave now · 2 min late"), and keep briefings quiet.
  - Turn the timeline pill warn/bad once leave-by has passed.
  - In kid mode, when the Next event's leave-by is within about 10 minutes, Right now becomes "Time to go!" with 👟 and the driver's face.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify (with /impeccable bolder for the banner)

**[P1] Phone Today still buries the day under the hub's hero**
- **What:** a 64px clock, the date, the Tomorrow link and four 64px faces push the first event to about 1290 of 1688 device pixels. The chips fixed the alert pile; the hero is now the obstacle.
- **Fix:** under 520px, fold the clock into the date line, shrink the faces to a 40px strip, and start with the chips and the next event.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Read-only things styled as toggles; Delete beside Edit**
- **What:**
  - The event sheet's attendee pill and the plan sheet's per-person progress (PlanSheet.tsx:145, `span.tog` with `aria-pressed`) look and announce as pressed buttons but do nothing.
  - Delete in the event sheet carries the same weight as Edit.
- **Fix:**
  - Show attendees as Face + name, and progress as plain chips. Drop `aria-pressed` from non-controls.
  - Move Delete behind Edit, or keep the ConfirmButton but quieter.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

**[P2] Every Got it on the hub needs a PIN**
- **What:** even "Today: 6 things on" shows 🔒 Got it. So on a busy morning briefings and packing banners sit over the board.
- **Fix:** let anyone on the hub answer informational nudges (morning, evening, reminders). Keep the PIN for leave-by, where answering stops escalation.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (parent on a phone):**
- Scrolls past a clock to reach the next event.
- Two stacked banners plus the + button cover about a third of the timeline.
- The late alert is a 16-digit number.

**Sam (keyboard and screen reader):**

- The chip counts vary with the title wording (Today.tsx:197 tests `/d/`, the letter d, instead of a digit), so some chips announce a count and others don't.

**Emma (5, can't read, on the kitchen tablet):**
- Later today shows written times with no clock face.
- At tile size the 🌙 moons look like bananas in the tablet's emoji font.
- Helping and countdown cards fall below the fold at 800px while the left column is empty.
- "Free time!" shows during a time-to-leave moment.
