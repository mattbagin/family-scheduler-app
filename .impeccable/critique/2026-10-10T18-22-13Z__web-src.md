## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** specific and strong. Things a generic calendar template could not carry:
- People own the color, with faces in rings.
- Sticker tiles stamp a ★.
- Kid mode uses analog clock faces and moons in fives.
- The Tomorrow board answers "Who's driving Emma to Swim lesson?" in place.
- The bedside night is complete.

The weak spots are fidelity gaps against DESIGN.md, not a lack of identity:
- On the hub's Today, the chore chart (the "heart") is the smallest thing.
- Night leaks green through kid clock faces.
- The leave chip breaks the one-clock rule.

**Deterministic scan:** the CLI found no warnings and 57 advisories. All are documented exceptions:
- picture, big-number and display sizes;
- night blacks and Tint Ink;
- ambient scenes.

In the browser, Month, Tomorrow and Settings were clean. Known false positives: the approved stamp curve, the recurring-event repeat, and the documented now-dot. **One real issue:** kid mode's sleeps number renders at 18px in marigold mixed 80% with Tint Ink, which is 4.3:1. That fails AA for normal-size text (DESIGN.md promises person colors as text pass AA).

## What's Working

1. **Leave-time honesty.** The pill and banner share one live clock: green, then amber "2 min late", then red "8 min late". Nothing flashes.
2. **Kid mode for pre-readers.** Clock faces, the face of who takes you, moons in fives, ☀️/🌙 on packing tiles, and a quiet note for grown-ups.
3. **The Tomorrow board and bedside night.** "Who's driving Emma to Swim lesson? [Mom][Dad]" is the best interaction in the app.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Quick add books into the past** *(pre-existing, Milestone 3)*
- **What:** "Leo swim Saturday 10am" typed on Saturday at 2 PM becomes "Today · 10:00 AM". `nextWeekday` counts today whatever the time.
- **Why it matters:** the event never appears on Today, and no nudge fires.
- **Fix:** pass the current minute in; a weekday that's today with a time already gone rolls to next week. The preview flags any start in the past.
- **Command:** /impeccable harden

**[P1] The sticker chart is the smallest thing on the hub**
- **What:** on Today the chart-row tiles render at about 97×66px, with a 1.6rem emoji and 12px labels (styles.css:300–304). DESIGN.md says 104px+ tiles and a 2–3.2rem emoji.
- **Why it matters:** the product's "heart" reads as a footnote beside a 5.6rem clock, and small hands get the smallest targets.
- **Fix:** honor 104px tiles and a 2.2rem+ emoji on the hub. Give the chart a full-width row on the hub if the side column is too narrow.
- **Command:** /impeccable bolder (or /impeccable layout)

**[P1] Banners, toasts and the page bottom collide**
- **What:** the toast sits at the banners' height (styles.css:529), so "Got it" feedback covers the next banner. The page keeps only 96px clear, so two or three stacked banners cover the last rows (packing on Tomorrow, timeline on phones).
- **Fix:**
  - Put the toast above the banner stack.
  - Pad the page by the stack's real height.
  - On phones, roll more than one banner into "3 nudges ▾".
- **Command:** /impeccable layout

**[P2] The one-clock rule is broken by the chip, and reminder banners go stale**
- **What:** "Leave in 53 min for Playdate" is an amber chip (Today.tsx:94, amber within 90 min) while the same ride's pill is green "leave 3:10 PM". Reminder banners keep their creation-time title ("in 15 min").
- **Fix:** keep the leave chip white (information) until leave time, worded like the pill. Recompute reminder titles from the event, as leave-by banners do.
- **Command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Night leaks color into kid clocks; kid clocks are silent to screen readers; sleeps number contrast**
- **What:**
  - ClockFace strokes its ring with a literal color (ui.tsx:294), which the night override can't reach.
  - The Now/Next clocks have no time text for screen readers.
  - The sleeps number is 4.3:1 at 18px.
- **Fix:**
  - Stroke with `var(--pc)`.
  - Add sr-only times beside the clocks.
  - Darken the sleeps number mix, or make it large text.
- **Command:** /impeccable polish
